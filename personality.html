LLMs: A Tool, Not a Crutch

To me, using AI as a tool means allowing it to support my learning without letting it do all my thinking for me. I can use AI to explain difficult concepts, quiz me before a test, guide me in doing various tasks, or help me identify errors in my codes.

However, AI can provide incorrect or biased information, and sharing personal information with these tools can create privacy risks and would be against the Data Protection Act. Constant usage of AI can prevent me from developing my own skills.

To use AI responsibly, I will verify information using reliable sources, test my code, and acknowledge sources when necessary. My personal rule is to use AI to help me understand and improve my work, not to complete it for me.
